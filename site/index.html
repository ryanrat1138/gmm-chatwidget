Chat embed setup

Adds the CloseBot AI chat to a page with one tag. On the thank-you page it fills in the visitor's name, email and phone from the form they just sent, so the chat starts on their existing HighLevel contact.

1Thank-you page: the chat

In CloseBot, open your HighLevel source and go to Setup. Paste the whole script tag here; the ID is the part after source=. Step-by-step: find your source ID

How the chat shows

Shown as the bot's first message when the chat would otherwise open empty, because the visitor's details were sent for them. {first_name} becomes their first name, or "there" if it isn't known. Leave it empty for none. If you set a Welcome Message in CloseBot, that shows instead.

Names of form answers passed in the thank-you page address or asked on your own form, separated by commas. They go to the AI along with the visitor's first chat message, so it has them right away, even before HighLevel has the contact's fields. The visitor doesn't see them; they do show in the HighLevel conversation. Step-by-step: what to put here

Call to action on the page

{first_name} becomes their first name; with no name, "You're all set, {first_name}!" reads "You're all set!". It uses the style settings below and your CloseBot color unless you pick a brand color.
