Add the AI chat to your thank-you page

Adds the CloseBot AI chat to a page with one tag. On the thank-you page it fills in the visitor's name, email and phone from the form they just sent, so the chat starts on their existing HighLevel contact.

Which chat window?

Use CloseBot's widgetCloseBot's own chat window, with your colors. The setup we've been using. Use our widget NewOur own chat window that talks to CloseBot directly. Replies stream in, and there's no CloseBot form to fill.

Step 1 of 5

Connect your CloseBot source

The chat runs on CloseBot. Paste your source's script tag here, then check three settings in CloseBot. Connecting your CloseBot account is optional.

In CloseBot, open your HighLevel source and go to Setup. Paste the whole script tag here; the ID is the part after source=. Step-by-step: find your source ID

Connect your CloseBot account (optional, uses your CloseBot API key)

Not needed for the chat itself. With it, the form answers from the next step are added to the visitor's conversation as an internal note that only the AI can see, as soon as the thank-you page opens. The visitor's first message then goes exactly as they typed it. Without this, the answers ride along with their first message instead.

  1. Sign in to CloseBot as the account owner and open Settings › API Keys.
  2. Click + at the top of the table, name the key (for example Chat embed) and create it.
  3. Copy the key straight away. CloseBot shows it only once.
  4. Paste it below, enter your website's address and click Connect CloseBot.

The address of the site the thank-you page is on, starting with https://. Separate several sites with commas. The key only works from these sites.

CloseBot checks the key, then it's locked to your sites and added to the code in scrambled form. It isn't stored anywhere, and it can't be read back out of your page. Step-by-step: connect CloseBot

In CloseBot

  1. Turn on the widget's formIn the chat widget settings, open Form, turn it on, include Email and Phone, and set it to show immediately.
  2. Target the thank-you pageTarget the widget to the thank-you page URL only, so CloseBot doesn't show it on the form page either.
  3. Allow your domainPut the site's domain on the source's allowed domains. CloseBot ignores pages on domains that aren't listed.

The embed fills and sends that form for the visitor. If it can't find their details, they fill it in themselves as usual.
