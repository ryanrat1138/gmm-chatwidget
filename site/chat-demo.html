Our own chat window for CloseBot

This page loads /chat.js, the chat we draw ourselves, connected to the live CloseBot source 4ts0AHLEWQsK5N84. Open it with the button in the corner.

What to try

  • Open the chat: with no details known it asks for your name and email or phone first, then the conversation starts on that HighLevel contact.
  • Add ?email=you@example.com&first_name=You to this page's address: the chat skips the form, greets you by name, and the details vanish from the address bar.
  • Reload after chatting: the conversation comes back.
  • The browser console shows what the widget did (data-debug="true"), never the values.

If the chat doesn't appear

CloseBot only answers sites on the source's allowed domains. Add gmm-chatwidget.vercel.app there, or try the tag on your own site. The older /embed.js tag is unchanged and keeps working.
